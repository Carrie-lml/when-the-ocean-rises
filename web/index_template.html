Introduction — Tuvalu in Context

A nation of nine low-lying islands and atolls, where most land lies only a few metres above sea level.

The New Zealand Connection

Tuvalu's 2022–23 Census recorded __TUVALU_RESIDENT_2223__ residents. New Zealand's 2023 Census counted __NZ_TUVALUAN_2023__ usual residents identifying with Tuvaluan ethnicity.

The Pacific Access Category, established in 2002, is a ballot-based New Zealand residence pathway for eligible Tuvalu citizens. For the 2026 ballot, __PAC_TUVALU_2026__ residence places were available to Tuvalu citizens. Separately, New Zealand’s 2023 Census counted __NZ_TUVALUAN_2023__ usual residents who identified with Tuvaluan ethnicity. Ethnicity is self-identified and is not the same as citizenship or country of birth. These data describe the size of a trans-Pacific community; they do not establish why individuals migrated.

Why This Matters to New Zealand

Stats NZ recorded __NZ_TUVALUAN_2013__ people identifying with Tuvaluan ethnicity in 2013 and __NZ_TUVALUAN_2023__ in 2023 — an increase of about __NZ_GROWTH_2013_2023__%. In 2023, __AUCKLAND_SHARE_2023__% of the Tuvaluan ethnic group had their usual residence in the Auckland region. Census counts are subject to Stats NZ’s fixed random rounding, so individual figures may not sum exactly.

Home — Future & Reflection

Rising seas raise increasingly urgent questions about low-lying Pacific homes.

Where — and how — can people continue to call home?

Tuvalu remains home, while Tuvaluan communities in Aotearoa sustain family, language and identity across the Pacific. This demographic connection is important context, but the census data do not identify climate change as the reason individuals moved.

Data & methodology

This project presents illustrative elevation-threshold exposure under selected relative sea-level increments. It is not an engineering flood model, a hydrodynamic inundation model, or a prediction of future shoreline change.

Scope: spatial exposure modelling is limited to Funafuti. Tuvalu's 2022–23 Census recorded __FUNAFUTI_RESIDENT_2223__ residents in Funafuti by region of enumeration, __FUNAFUTI_SHARE_2223__% of the national resident population. The other eight islands and atolls are not modelled individually.

Scenario interpretation: +0.5 m, +1.0 m, +1.5 m and +2.0 m are illustrative elevation thresholds, not year-specific forecasts. The observed 1993–2023 SPC sea-level anomaly series is a separate evidence stream and is not mathematically converted into these thresholds.

Model baseline: the __BASELINE_AREA_KM2__ km² baseline is the summed area of valid ASTER DEM pixels above 0 m within the Funafuti study-area clip; it is not Funafuti's official land area. Approximately __BASELINE_POPULATION__ modelled people overlap those DEM-positive pixels. The native 2020 WorldPop Funafuti clip totals approximately __NATIVE_WORLDPOP_TOTAL__ modelled people.

Population processing: WorldPop was reprojected to the ASTER grid using nearest-neighbour resampling. NoData values were excluded, and the aligned population values were rescaled so that their total matched the native clipped WorldPop total before exposure was calculated.

Limitations: the simplified threshold method does not model tides, waves or wave run-up, storm surge, groundwater flooding, erosion, drainage, coastal defences, hydrodynamic connectivity or future shoreline geomorphology. ASTER GDEM has substantial vertical uncertainty over a very low, narrow coral-atoll environment. ASTER elevations are stored in whole metres, so identical adjacent threshold results can reflect DEM quantisation; the 0.5 m slider increments do not imply 0.5 m DEM precision. Results should therefore be interpreted as screening-level elevation exposure rather than precise flood extents. The LiDAR elevation statistics shown in the introduction are used only as an external topographic reference; the threshold analysis itself uses ASTER GDEM.

Population context: WorldPop 2020 is a modelled population estimate, not a census headcount. Its native 2020 Funafuti clip totals approximately __NATIVE_WORLDPOP_TOTAL__ modelled people, close to Funafuti's 2017 resident population count of __FUNAFUTI_RESIDENT_2017__. This agreement is used as a reasonableness check only and does not imply that the datasets are equivalent.
